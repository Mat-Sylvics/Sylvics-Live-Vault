# Start here

Welcome. Sylvics is one vault for your whole life: what you plan, what you learn, how your days go and how each part of your life is doing. Everything you write in it ends up somewhere useful, on its own.

This vault arrives full. Every example note belongs to **Mira Castellan**, an invented person who used Sylvics for all of 2025, so every page already shows something. You don't need to learn anything before you start. Twenty minutes and four small steps are enough to try the whole loop.

```sylvics-view
FirstWeekCard
```

**Finding your way.** At the top of a full-screen page, **←** goes back, **UP** goes one page up and **⛶** brings Obsidian's sidebars back. **Cmd/Ctrl + P** runs any Sylvics action by name, and **Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + H** takes you home. If you get lost, **🚀 Start here** at the top of the Control Center brings you back to this page.

## 1. Write today's note (2 minutes)

Press `Cmd/Ctrl + D`. Today's note opens, created for you. Type one line under **The ONE outcome that makes today a win**. That's a full first day.

```sylvics-button
Open today's note | sylvics-core:open-today-daily-note
```

**Why it matters:** the daily note feeds almost everything else. Your sleep, habits, mood, spending and the people you saw are read from it by the dashboards, so the vault fills up as you live, with no extra bookkeeping.

## 2. Catch a thought (1 minute)

Open [[01. Braindump]], type whatever is on your mind (a task, a worry, an idea) and click **Send to the inbox →** or press `Cmd/Ctrl + Enter`. The first line becomes its name.

```sylvics-button
Open the Braindump | [[01. Braindump]]
```

**Why it matters:** a thought you've written down stops taking up room in your head. Capture takes seconds because sorting comes later.

## 3. Sort the inbox (3 minutes)

Open [[02. Inbox Processing]]. It shows one note at a time, oldest first. Mira's ten jots are waiting ahead of yours, so click yours in **The queue** on the right.

```sylvics-button
Sort the inbox | [[02. Inbox Processing]]
```

Press `T` (or click **📌 Make a task**), keep or change the name, and click **Create task**. The task opens with your text in its **NOTES** box. The jot goes to the archive, never to the trash. Now open [[02. Mission Deck]] and click the **📌 Tasks** tab: your task is there, next to Mira's.

The other keys are `P` for a project, `M` to move the note to a folder, `D` to delete it and `J`/`K` to step through the queue.

**Why it matters:** this is the loop the whole system runs on. You capture anything, decide once what it is, and find it where you work.

## 4. See where this leads (3 minutes)

Here is what a few months of steps 1 to 3 look like.

- **One of Mira's days:** [[2025-10-24-Friday]]. Her intention, habits, people, money and a short summary. Use **← Yesterday** and **Tomorrow →** to walk through her week.
- **Her week in review:** [[2025-W43]]. Each life area gets a score from 1 to 10, and she writes one line about it. Fifteen minutes on a Sunday.
- **Her whole year at once:** the graph draws every note and how they connect. **Arbor**, on the left, shows it as a tree, one bough per life area. Press `Space` to watch the vault grow from its first note, and `H` to come back.

```sylvics-button
Open Mira's Friday | [[2025-10-24-Friday]]
Open her week | [[2025-W43]]
Open the graph | sylvics-core:neural-core-open-graph
```

> [!note] Why the dashboards say it's December 2025
> While the examples are in, dashboards treat 31 December 2025 as today, so Mira's year looks alive. Your own notes always carry the real date. When you remove the examples, the dashboards switch to the real date.

## 5. Your first weekly review (at the end of the week)

When the week ends, open its review. Score each area and write one line about it.

```sylvics-button
Open this week's review | sylvics-core:open-this-week-review
```

After a few weeks, each life area page shows its trend: which weeks went well, which didn't, and what was going on.

## Next steps, when you're ready

- **Too much at once?** [[🌱 Choose what you see]] hides the parts you don't need yet. Nothing is deleted, and you can bring them back with one click.
- **Back up before you rely on this vault.** Until you do, your notes exist only on this computer. See [[11 Settings, licence, backup and updates]].
- **Remove Mira when she has done her job.** [[Example Files Index]] removes the examples one at a time, by folder, or all at once. What you wrote is never touched.
- **Look something up.** [[00 Guide index]] lists every guide page. [[Cheat sheet]] fits on one page.
- **Something looks wrong?** If a page shows grey code boxes instead of a dashboard, the plugins aren't running yet: see [[12 Troubleshooting and FAQ]]. To report a problem, see [[💬 About this beta]].

When you no longer need this page, delete it. The **Start Here** card on the Control Center goes with it, and the guide stays.
