# The example files

After this page you will know who the examples belong to, how to learn from them, and how to remove them group by group or all at once.

## Who Mira is

Every example note belongs to one invented person. **Mira Castellan** is 34 and a senior product manager at a logistics software company. She writes a newsletter every two weeks, takes an evening statistics course, lifts three mornings a week, climbs on Saturdays and is saving for a sabbatical.

Mira, her city, her employer, her friends and every shop she visits are invented. The books, articles, films, podcasts and studies she takes notes on are real works. The notes about them are written in her words. The cover pictures on her sources are plain covers drawn for this vault.

[[About the example files]] has the short version of this page.

## What is there

- **About 1,800 example notes** across every note type, each marked with the property `demo: true`.
- **A full journal for 2025**: 365 daily notes, 53 weekly reviews, 12 monthly, 4 quarterly and one yearly review, from 1 January to 31 December 2025.
- **123 cover pictures** for her sources, **234 pixel-art pictures** for recipes, ingredients, goals, places and other notes, **18 lecture handouts** (PDF), **8 ambient tracks** and **578 photographs**: in the galleries of 105 notes, on 184 daily notes, and as the covers of the 20 exercise and 20 workout-routine notes. The photographs show real places, also where the note carries a fictional name. Their sources are in [[Credits and sources]].

The notes link to each other on purpose. A day links to its tasks, people and books. A book links to its literature notes. Those link to the permanent notes they fed. Following links is the fastest way to understand how the vault is meant to work.

## The year is 2025

Mira's dates are fixed in 2025. So that her year still looks alive, the dashboards treat **31 December 2025** as today for as long as example notes exist: "this week" is the last week of her year, "due today" is what she had due on that day. A bar at the top of each of these dashboards says so.

| You want to | Do this |
|---|---|
| See your own "today" and "this week" while her notes are still there | **Use today's date** on that bar, or `Cmd/Ctrl + P` → **Sylvics Core: Toggle example dates** |
| Go back to her year | **Show the example year** on the same bar |

Notes you create always get the real date. When the last example note is deleted, or kept as your own, the dashboards use today's date by themselves and the bar is gone.

## Keep them for a while

There is no need to delete anything in the first weeks. Your notes and Mira's can sit side by side. The examples are most useful exactly when you are unsure how to fill a page: open hers.

A reasonable rhythm: when a section has stopped teaching you anything, delete that section's examples.

## Remove the examples

Open [[Example Files Index]], or run `Cmd/Ctrl + P` → **Sylvics Core: Open Example Files Index**.

The page lists everything marked as an example, grouped by top folder and then by note type, with a count on each group. The line at the top says how many notes and attachments remain. Use the search box to filter by name, folder or type.

| You want to | Do this |
|---|---|
| Look at an example | Click its name |
| Delete one note | **Delete** on its row, then **Sure?** |
| Delete a group | **Delete this group** on the group's row. The button changes to a question with the number of notes. Click it again to confirm. |
| Delete what a search found | Type in the search box, then **Delete these** on a group |
| Keep a note as your own | **Keep as mine**. The example mark is removed and the note leaves the list. Do this for any example you have edited and want to keep. |
| Delete everything | **Delete all examples**. A dialog states the number of notes and attachments. Type `DELETE` and click **Delete all examples** (or press Enter). The two switches in that dialog are explained below. |
| Stop a deletion in progress | **Stop** |

### The two switches of "Delete all examples"

| Switch | On (as it comes) | Off |
|---|---|---|
| **Keep the example notes you have changed** | Examples you edited after the vault arrived are left out of the deletion. They stay in the list: press **Keep as mine** on the ones you want, or delete them afterwards. | They are deleted with the others |
| **Also clear Mira's settings from the system pages** | Her balances and budgets, her training week, her timetable entry and the sample portfolios are emptied, and her bank accounts are replaced by two neutral ones, **Main account** and **Cash**, so the finance and planning pages start blank and logging works at once | The pages keep her values until you replace them by hand |

The first switch appears only when a changed example exists, the second only while one of those pages still holds her values. A value is cleared only if it is still exactly as it came. An account or a budget you have already changed is yours and stays.

Deleting on a large vault takes a few minutes. The bar under the count shows the progress, and **Stop** ends it after the current file.

### Where deleted files go

The page tells you, under the count. It follows your Obsidian setting **Settings → Files and links → Deleted files**: your computer's trash, a `.trash` folder inside the vault, or permanent deletion. With either trash you can get the files back. Check the setting before a large deletion.

### What is never touched

- Your own notes. A note counts as an example only when its properties contain `demo: true`.
- The dashboards, masterpages and templates.
- This guide.

### What stays behind, and needs you

- **Mira's settings on system pages.** [[7. Finance Settings]] and [[1. Live Account Balances]] hold her accounts, budgets and balances so that her budgets work; [[2. Training plan]] holds her training week and [[2. Timetable]] her evening class; the three wealth pages hold a sample portfolio. These pages are not deleted. **Delete all examples** empties those values for you (second switch). If you deleted the examples group by group, the page that says **No examples left** offers the same under **Clear them**. Categories, colours and the currency are kept: change them in [[7. Finance Settings]].
- **Empty folders.** The dated journal folders and her course folders are removed once they are empty. The folders for each note type stay, empty and ready for your notes.
- **Links in this guide and in [[🚀 Start Here]]** that point at her notes stop working. The instructions still apply.
- **Pages without notes.** A list with nothing in it says what it is for and offers its create button. Scores and rates show a dash until there is something to measure, and a chart shows a short line of text in place of empty axes. Nothing is broken: the page is waiting for your first note.
- **The graph and Sage's index** update on their own after a deletion. If the graph still shows old points, run **Sylvics Core: Force Refresh**.

## Getting them back

If the examples went to a trash, restore them from there. Otherwise, copy them again from the original download: unpack it to a separate folder and copy the notes you want into the same places in your vault. Do not unpack the download over your vault. That would overwrite pages you have changed.
