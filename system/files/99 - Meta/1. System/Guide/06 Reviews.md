# Reviews

After this page you can start a weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly review and you will know what each is for.

> [!tip] The rule behind this page
> **The Weekly Heartbeat**: one review a week keeps the Inbox clear, the projects honest and the area scores current. All ten rules: [[The rules of the vault]].

## The five levels

| Review | How long | The question it answers | Mira's example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily note | 5 to 10 minutes | What matters today, and what happened? | [[2025-10-24-Friday]] |
| Weekly | 20 to 30 minutes | What did this week show, and what are next week's three priorities? | [[2025-W20]] |
| Monthly | 30 to 45 minutes | Where did the money, the time and the attention go? | [[2025-05-May]] |
| Quarterly | About an hour | Are my goals still the right goals? | [[2025-Q2]] |
| Yearly | An afternoon | What was this year, and what is the next one for? | [[2025]] |

Start with the daily note and the weekly review. Add the monthly review after a month. The quarterly and yearly reviews can wait until the calendar brings them.

Mira's journal has every one of these for 2025: 365 days, 53 weeks, 12 months, 4 quarters and the year. Reading two of her weekly reviews teaches the form faster than any description.

## How to start a review

Reviews are created the first time you open them, each from its own template, into `07 - Journal`.

| Review | Fastest way to open or create it | File created |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly | In the Sylvics calendar, click the **W** button with the week number, bottom left. Or click **This Week** at the top of any daily note of that week. | `02 Weekly/<year>/<year>-W<week>.md` |
| Monthly | Click **This Month** at the top of a weekly review. | `03 Monthly/<year>/<year>-<month number>-<month name>.md` |
| Quarterly | In the calendar, click the **Q** button with the quarter number, bottom right. Or click **This Quarter** at the top of a monthly review. | `04 Quarterly/<year>/<year>-Q<number>.md` |
| Yearly | Click **This Year** at the top of a quarterly review. | `05 Yearly/<year>.md` |

The calendar opens with `Cmd/Ctrl + P` → **Open Sylvics Calendar**. [[02 Your day]] has the details. The **W** and **Q** buttons show the week and quarter numbers they will open. If you have moved the calendar to another month, click **TODAY** first, so that they point at the current week and quarter again.

Each review has **Previous** and **Next** buttons at the top to move along its own level.

## The weekly review

A weekly review is one page in five parts. The page fills the first three from your notes. You write the last two.

| Part | Heading | What it shows or asks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **RETROSPECTIVE** | The seven days as tabs, each day's rating, pictures, and a box headed **WHAT HAPPENED THIS WEEK?** |
| 2 | **KNOWLEDGE FLOW** | What entered or moved this week, on tabs: Inbox, Resources, Literature, Permanent, MOCs |
| 3 | **ACTION FLOW** | Goals, Projects, Tasks and Latent Ideas touched this week |
| 4 | **SYSTEM REFLECTION** | Your written reflection, and the **Areas Standard Checks** |
| 5 | **PRIMING** | Next week's priorities, and a short closing checklist |

A sequence that works:

1. **Empty the Inbox first.** Open [[02. Inbox Processing]] and deal with every jot: make it a task or a project, file it, or delete it. A jot you turn into a task or a project is kept in `08 - Archive/1. Inbox`.
2. Read the seven day tabs in part 1. Write five lines under **WHAT HAPPENED THIS WEEK?**.
3. Glance at parts 2 and 3. They need no writing. Notice what stalled.
4. In part 4, answer what you can: the most storyworthy moment, the most valuable thing learnt, three wins, three misses, and **Did I act like the person I want to become?**
5. Under **Areas Standard Checks**, give each of the eight areas a number from 1 to 10. These numbers become the scores on the area pages. See [[04 Your life areas]].
6. In part 5, write **the 3 non-negotiable priorities for next week**, then tick the closing checklist.

**Short on time?** The fifteen-minute version is steps 1, 5 and 6: empty the Inbox, give each area its number, write the three priorities. Do the rest when you can.

Part 4 also contains a card titled **Sage Intelligence Protocol** with a button, **⚡ Open Sage Chat**. Sage works on demand: the button opens the Sage chat, where you can ask for a review of this week's journal. Nothing runs in the background. See [[09 Sage]].

## The monthly review

The monthly review adds the numbers a week cannot show: **Financial Telemetry** (from the daily ledgers), **Nutritional Telemetry**, the month's ratings, the weeks as tabs, **Monthly Category Averages** and **Wheel of Life Progression**, which draws your weekly area numbers as a line over the month. Then it asks for a short reflection and next month's focus.

The card **🛠️ VAULT HYGIENE** on the same page has the button **RUN SYSTEM UPDATES**. It asks whether your community plugins and your installed themes have newer versions and lists the ones that do, plugins first and themes second, each with the installed and the new version. Nothing is installed until you click **Update all**; **Not now** closes the box. Without an internet connection the box says **No connection** and offers **Try again**; nothing is changed. If this version of Obsidian does not allow the check at all, Obsidian's **Community plugins** settings open instead. The command **Check for plugin and theme updates** (`Cmd/Ctrl + P`) does the same from anywhere. Sylvics itself is not updated this way: see [[11 Settings, licence, backup and updates]].

## The quarterly and yearly reviews

Both follow the same form at a wider angle: ratings across the period, then the months (or quarters) to look back through, then reflection. Use the quarterly review to open each goal on [[🎯 0. Goals Masterpage]] and decide: keep, change, pause or archive. Use the yearly review to read your four quarterly reviews in a row and write what the year was.

## If you fall behind

A missed week is a missed week, and so is a missed month. Do not fill in old reviews. Do the Return instead:

1. Open this week's review.
2. Empty the Inbox. A jot you cannot decide on now can be moved to Ideas & Explorations with **📁 Move** and wait there.
3. Open [[📂 0. Projects Masterpage]] once. Archive any project you have dropped; the rest can stay as they are.
4. Write the three priorities and open today's note.

That is all. The system is built to be picked up again.

## Finding old reviews

[[00. Journal Masterpage]] lists your daily notes with their numbers. [[03. Life Review]] has tabs for the latest daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and annual notes. You can also browse `07 - Journal` in the file explorer: the folders are `01 Daily`, `02 Weekly`, `03 Monthly`, `04 Quarterly` and `05 Yearly`.
