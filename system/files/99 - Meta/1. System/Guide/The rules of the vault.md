# The rules of the vault

After this page you will know the ten rules behind where things go and how they move. You do not need to learn them by heart. The guide pages name the rule they follow, so you will meet each one where it applies.

## Above the rules

**One golden rule.** If something here creates more friction than it removes, simplify it. Lite mode, the Simple switch on area pages and the areas you leave alone all exist for this.

**Three keystones.** These are the only things the vault asks of you.

1. **One place.** If it matters, it goes in the vault. If you are not sure where, it goes in the Inbox.
2. **Clear the Inbox once a week.** Make each jot a task or a project, file it, or let it go. Missed a few weeks? See "If you fall behind" in [[06 Reviews]].
3. **Finish thoughts in your own words.** What you keep as knowledge, you restate yourself.

Everything below is a habit the vault helps you with. Nothing breaks if you bend one.

## Where a note lives

| Rule | In one line | Example from Mira's vault |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The Use Rule** | A note lives where it will be used, not where it came from. | A recipe is reference, so it lives in `04 - Resources`. The plan to cook four nights a week is a habit in `02 - Planning`. Last night's dinner is a line in the daily note. |
| **2. The Finish Line Test** | If you can finish it, it goes in `02 - Planning`. If you can only keep it up, it is an area in `03 - Areas`. | [[🎯 Fill the sabbatical fund to 18,000 euros]] has a finish line, so it is a goal. Money in general has none, so it is the Finances area. |
| **3. The Authorship Test** | Someone else's words go in `04 - Resources`. Their idea in your words goes in `05 - Literature`. Your own idea goes in `06 - Permanent`. | The book [[📕 Continuous Discovery Habits]], her account of it in [[📖 Torres on opportunity trees]], and the idea she now holds, [[💎 An opportunity is a customer problem and not a feature in disguise]]. |
| **4. Warehouse and Workshop** | Store a thing once in `04 - Resources`. The area pages show it wherever you need it. | Her contacts, such as [[Jonas Reuter]], live in Resources. [[1. Personal CRM]] in the Relationships area lists them; nothing is copied. |
| **5. When in Doubt, Inbox** | If you cannot decide yet, do not decide yet. | Her ten jots wait in `01 - Inbox` until the weekly review. |

## How the folders fit together

**6. Three modes, three services.** At any moment you are learning, doing or reviewing. Each kind of work has its folders, and three more folders support you.

| You are... | Folders |
|---|---|
| Doing | `02 - Planning`, `03 - Areas` |
| Learning | `04 - Resources`, `05 - Literature`, `06 - Permanent` |
| Reviewing | `07 - Journal` |
| **See**: looking at everything | `00 - Atlas` |
| **Catch**: capturing | `01 - Inbox` |
| **Keep**: storing what is finished | `08 - Archive` |
| The engine room | `99 - Meta` |

The modes describe you. The folders are where each mode's work lands.

## How notes move

| Rule | In one line | Where you see it |
|---|---|---|
| **7. One Door In** | Everything you capture enters through the Inbox. | [[01. Braindump]] sends to the Inbox. |
| **8. Born in Place** | Create a note from the masterpage of its kind. The button picks the folder and the layout. | Every masterpage has one create button. You rarely choose a folder yourself. |
| **9. Status Is Location** | Set a planning note to its archived status and it moves to `08 - Archive` by itself. Set it back and it returns. | Tasks, projects, goals and the rest of `02 - Planning`. See [[03 Planning]]. |

## How the vault stays true

**10. The Weekly Heartbeat.** One review a week keeps everything else true: the Inbox is cleared, projects are looked at, and the numbers you give the eight areas become the scores on their pages. See [[06 Reviews]].

## Four more you will notice

The vault follows other rules too. These four explain things you will see every day.

- **The leverage gradient.** The sections of `04 - Resources` run from quick help to deep study: tools, reference, people and places, short facts and concepts, then whole sources. Go only as deep as the problem in front of you needs.
- **Flat where thinking happens.** `05 - Literature` and `06 - Permanent` have no subfolders. Your ideas are organised by the links between them, and by topic maps in `00 - Atlas`.
- **The type badge.** The emoji at the start of a note's name tells you its type: 📌 task, 📂 project, 🎯 goal, 📖 literature note, 💎 permanent note. Keep it when you rename a note.
- **The thread up.** Every note names one parent, so the **UP** button at the top of a full-screen page always leads somewhere.
