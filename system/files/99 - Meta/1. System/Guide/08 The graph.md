# The graph

After this page you can open the graph, read each of its views, find what you are looking for in it, and save a picture or a short film of your vault.

## What it is

The graph draws your vault. Every mark stands for something real in your notes, and each view has a legend that says what its shapes, sizes and colours mean. Where a view adds something only for beauty (the bend of a branch, the stars in the sky), the legend says so under **Decoration**.

It has two tabs:

- **Global views**: your whole vault as a picture: a tree, a forest, a sky, a globe, a card. Made to look at and to share, and still truthful.
- **Insights**: questions about your work, answered with charts and plain sentences. Accuracy comes first here.

Notes of the system (the pages of this guide, masterpages, templates) are never counted or drawn. Neither are the notes you exclude under **Privacy & Paths** (see the end of this page).

## Open it

`Cmd/Ctrl + P` → **Open Sylvics Canopy Graph**, or the graph button in the ribbon. It opens as a full-screen page. The first time, it waits until Obsidian has read the whole vault; the status line says how far it is.

The graph remembers the tab, the view and where you were looking, also after Obsidian restarts.

## The screen

| Part | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| View list | Left | The two tabs, **GLOBAL VIEWS** and **INSIGHTS**, and a card for each view. The arrow folds the list away. |
| Legend and findings | Right | What the picture means. In Insights, the findings come first: sentences with the notes behind them. |
| Timeline or period bar | Bottom | Play the growth of the vault, or choose the period the findings count (below). |
| Top bar | Top | **Search** (`/`), the **Filter** box, the camera button to share, Home (`H`) and the settings of the graph. |

### Move around

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Turn | Drag |
| Move sideways | Drag with the right button |
| Zoom | Scroll |
| Back to the starting view | `H`, or the Home button |
| Focus on a note | Click it. The note's links light up, the rest dims, and a card shows the note. |
| Open the note | Double-click it, or press `Enter` while it is in focus |
| See what a mark is | Point at it: a small card shows its title, its kind and two facts |

In Insights the picture is flat: drag moves it and scroll zooms where the pointer is.

## Global views

| View | What it shows | How to read it | One thing to try |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Arbor** | Your vault as one tree, unique to this vault | Roots (violet, under the ground) are your sources and literature notes; the lights inside the trunk are your values, beliefs and other notes about who you are; each bough is a life area; branches follow your goals, projects and tasks. The leaves are notes: their colour is the kind of note, their size how many notes link to them, their brightness how recently you worked on them. Gold blossoms are permanent notes and completed goals, fruit are finished projects, bare buds are active work untouched for 30 days, fallen leaves are archived notes. Your journal days lie on the ground around the foot, one circle per month. | Press `Space` and watch the tree grow from your first note to today. |
| **Forest** | Your vault as a forest: one plant per subject | A plant is a group of notes that belong together (a life area's plan, or notes that link to each other). Its form comes from its mix of notes: tall light-trees for knowledge, shrubs for people, ferns for sources, ground cover for quotes and concepts, broad trees for the rest. The colour of the leaves is the kind of note, the tint of the wood is the life area, brightness is how much of the plant you worked on in the last 45 days; a grey plant is dormant. Roots of light on the ground join plants whose notes link. The stream is your journal, one light per day. | Click the light at the foot of a plant to light the whole plant. |
| **Cosmos** | Your vault as a sky | Each galaxy is a group of notes that link mostly to each other, placed near the galaxies it links to. A star is a note: its size is how many notes link to it, its colour the kind of note, its twinkle recent work. Light lanes join galaxies that link. The spiral in the middle is your journal, oldest day in the centre. Notes with no link at all form the faint outer belt. | Press `Space`: the stars light up in the order the notes were written. |
| **Orb** | Your folders on a globe | Each top-level folder is a sector of the globe; folders inside it are patches, deeper ones a little under the surface. A dot is a note, larger when more notes link to it. Threads are links. | In the graph settings, turn on **Light along the threads**. |
| **Signature** | A card about your vault, made to share | Your glyph (drawn from your vault: one vein per life area you write in), a small copy of your tree, four numbers and the date. The name on the card is the setting **Name on your card**. | Choose the card's shape in the graph settings, then save it with the camera button. |

## Insights

Each Insight has a chart and a **Findings** panel. A finding is a plain sentence with the notes behind it: click a note chip to show it in the picture, double-click to open it. Above the findings are a few headline numbers; some Insights add ranked lists.

| Insight | What it shows | How to read it | One thing to try |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plan** | Your goals, their projects and their tasks | A tree by life area: the areas in the middle, goals around them, projects further out, tasks at the edge. Open work is lit, finished work fades. Findings: what is late, what has had no activity for 30 days, projects with no goal, goals with no active project, goals with more than three active projects. | Switch the layout to **Timeline** to see every goal and project on a date axis, late ones marked. |
| **Habits** | Your year of habit ticks | One ring per habit, one cell per day; a filled cell is a day the habit was done. A habit counts only on the days it was listed in your daily note, so a weekly habit done every week is at 100%. Findings: streaks, best and weakest weekdays, habits rising or fading, habits done together, and days compared with your day rating (compared, not causes). | Switch the layout to **Rows** to read one habit at a time. |
| **Knowledge flow** | How reading becomes knowledge | Sources on the left, literature notes in the middle, permanent notes on the right. Every bar and band is as tall as its number of notes. Sources that gave no note stand apart. Findings: finished sources with no notes, literature notes waiting to become permanent notes, sources still in progress for a long time, the sources that gave the most. | Switch the layout to **By month** to see when you read and when you wrote. |
| **Structure** | The shape of your links | The same groups as the galaxies of Cosmos, as discs joined by lines (thicker = more links). Ringed notes are bridges: many paths between groups pass through them. Findings and lists: bridges, notes that would cut a group off if lost, notes with no link, notes untouched for six months that nothing links to, and pairs of notes worth linking. | Open the list of suggested links and link one pair. |
| **Balance** | Your life areas: how you rate them and the attention they get | A radar of the scores from your weekly (or monthly) reviews, the latest in bold, older ones fainter. Violet dots and the dashed violet shape are attention: notes of the area you worked on, tasks done and hours logged. Findings: areas rated low and neglected, areas that get effort while their score falls. | Switch the layout to **Rows** to follow each area over time. |
| **Days** | Your journal year as a river | The height of the river is your day rating, its width your energy, its colour your sleep (each a 7-day average; one dot per day). Trips are bars above it; finished goals and projects and events are marks under it. Findings: best and hardest weeks and what filled them, your journal habit, what goes with better days, the trend. | Change **River** in the graph settings to see other pairs of numbers. |
| **People** | Your people by real contact | Each ring is a time since you were last in touch, from this week in the middle to two years at the edge; sectors are your circles (family, friends, work…). A contact is a meeting, a call or a name in your daily contact list, not a mention in a note. Bigger means more contact in the last year, brighter means more contact lately than usual. Findings: people drifting away, long silences, upcoming birthdays. | Switch the layout to **Rows** to see each person's year of contact. |

## Time: play the growth, choose a period

**In Global views** the bar at the bottom is a timeline. Press `Space` (or the play button) and the vault grows from its first note to today: the tree grows, the forest plants itself, the stars light up, with the real date and the number of notes as it goes. Drag along the bar to go to a moment. Choose the speed and turn on the loop beside it.

The date of a note is the day it was created (the `created` property of every new note). For notes without it, the graph uses the date in the name of a daily note, another date of the note, or the first daily note that links to it.

**In Insights** the bar at the bottom is a period bar: small bars show your activity, day by day. Drag across them to choose the period the findings count, or pick **4W**, **3M**, **6M**, **1Y** or **All**. Marks outside the period dim. The reset button returns to the whole period. Facts about today (what is late, what is due) always count from today.

## Find something

**Search** (`/`): type part of a title. The list shows matching notes; `Enter` flies to the first one.

**Filter**: type in the **Filter** box. Matching notes stay lit, the rest dims, nothing moves. The filter works in every view.

| Type this | To keep |
|---|---|
| `area:health` | Notes of one life area |
| `type:task` | Notes of one kind (`type:book`, `type:literature`, `type:journal`, `type:knowledge`…) |
| `status:active` | Notes with one status (`inbox`, `active`, `waiting`, `done`, `dropped`, `archived`, or the status word of the note itself) |
| `tag:statistics` | Notes with a tag |
| `is:orphan` | Notes with no link at all (`is:linked` for the others) |
| `is:recent` | Notes you worked on in the last 30 days |
| `is:stale` | Notes untouched for more than 90 days |
| `is:open`, `is:done` | Unfinished or finished items |
| `is:example` | Mira's example notes |
| `is:estimated` | Notes whose date the graph had to guess |
| `after:2025-06` or `before:2025-03-15` | Notes created from or before a day, a month (`2025-06`) or a year (`2025`) |
| any other word | Notes with that word in the title or the path |

Several filters together must all match. A `-` in front leaves notes out: `type:task -is:done` keeps unfinished tasks. Example: `area:health is:orphan` shows health notes that nothing links to. `Esc` empties the filter.

## Share a picture or a film

The camera button opens the share dialog. On the left is the exact picture that will be saved.

- **Image** or **Clip**. A clip of a Global view is its growth from the first note to today; a view without a timeline turns slowly; an Insight moves in a little.
- **Format**: 16:9, 1:1, 4:5, 9:16 or the size of your window, at 1×, 2× or 4× (clips up to 2×). Clips last 6 to 20 seconds, at 30 or 60 frames per second.
- **Signature strip**: your glyph, the vault's name, the number of notes and days, the date and the Sylvics mark, each on or off. **Legend strip**: what the colours mean, along the top.
- **Note titles** are off every time the dialog opens. Turn them on only for a picture you keep to yourself: anyone who sees it can read them.
- Clips are saved as **MP4** (H.264), which plays in QuickTime and uploads to Instagram, TikTok and X. If your computer cannot make MP4, the dialog says so and saves a **WebM** file instead; VLC, Chrome and Obsidian play it.
- Files go to `99 - Meta/4. Attachments/4. Exports` in your vault, named after the view and the day. Change the folder with the setting **Exports folder**. Nothing is uploaded: the picture leaves your computer only if you send it.

A clip is drawn frame by frame, so it plays smoothly however long it takes to make. The graph rests while it works, and **Stop** cancels without saving.

## Keyboard

These work while the graph is the page you are looking at and you are not typing in a box.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `1` to `9` | Open the first nine views of the tab you are in |
| `Space` | Play or pause the growth |
| `F` | Focus the note under the pointer, or fly to the note in focus |
| `H` | Back to the starting view |
| `/` | Search for a note |
| `Enter` | Open the note in focus |
| `C` | Hide or show everything around the picture |
| `Esc` | Close the search, then the focus, then the hidden mode, then the filter, one at a time |

## Settings

The gear in the top bar holds the settings of the graph:

- **Quality**: **Low**, **Balanced** or **High**. Low draws fewer labels and no glow and is kindest to a laptop battery.
- **Labels**: **Off**, **Few**, **Normal** or **Many** note names.
- **Motion**: twinkling, the slowly drifting camera and the light along the lanes. Turn it off for a still picture.
- The options of the view you are in (layout, format, river…).
- **Hide interface** and **Classic graph**.

The graph stops drawing when its tab is hidden.

In **Settings → Sylvics Core Plugin → Visuals & UI**:

- **Use classic graph**: the graph command and the ribbon button open the earlier graph instead. The command **Open Sylvics Canopy Graph (Classic)** opens it at any time, whatever this setting says.
- **Name on your card**: the name on the Signature card and the signature strip. Empty means "My vault".
- **Exports folder**: where shared images and clips are saved.

## What the graph remembers about your work

To show real activity (and not the dates your files were copied or synced), the graph keeps a small log of which notes you edited on which day. It is stored in the plugin's own folder, `.obsidian/plugins/sylvics-core/data/activity-log.json`, never in a note, and never leaves your computer. It keeps three years and drops older days. A change to more than 25 notes at once (a sync, a find and replace, deleting the examples) is not counted as work. Notes excluded under **Privacy & Paths** are never written to it.

## Mira's notes and dates

Every example note carries the day Mira created it, so the growth, the activity and the Insights tell the story of her year in 2025.

## Notes you do not want in the graph

Templates, attachments and the system pages are left out. To leave out your own notes, add their folder or tag under **Settings → Sylvics Core Plugin → Privacy & Paths**. An excluded note is never drawn, counted, searched or written to the activity log. The same lists hide notes from Sage. See [[09 Sage]].

## If something looks wrong

- **The graph stays on "Reading your vault"**: Obsidian is still indexing after a large change. Wait for it; the status line counts up.
- **Old points after deleting notes**: run **Sylvics Core: Force Refresh**.
- **"Picture paused"**: the graphics driver took the picture away (after sleep, or with many 3D views open). It comes back by itself; if not, close the graph and open it again.
- **"Graphics unavailable"**: the graph needs WebGL 2. Check that hardware acceleration is on in Obsidian (**Settings → Appearance**). Every other page works without it.
- **It is slow**: choose **Low** quality and turn off **Motion** in the graph's settings, and close the graph tab when you are done.
