# Your life areas

After this page you will know what each of the eight areas holds and what to try first in each.

> [!tip] The rule behind this page
> **The Finish Line Test** and **Warehouse and Workshop**: an area is what you keep up and never finish, and its pages show material stored once in Resources. All ten rules: [[The rules of the vault]].

## What an area is

An area is a part of life you keep up with no finish line: your health, your money, your home. A project ends. An area does not. If you can finish it, it belongs in `02 - Planning`. If you can only keep it at a standard, it is an area.

[[0. Areas Masterpage]] shows the eight areas with a score for each and their active goals. Each area has its own masterpage, and under it a set of tool pages built for that part of life.

## How an area page works

Every area masterpage has the same layout, so once you know one you know all eight:

- **At the top, without scrolling:** the area's name and score, and one card for each of its rooms (for Health: Nutrition, Fitness, Medical). Each card shows one live line from its notes, such as the next follow-up or the last workout.
- **The score** is the number from 1 to 10 you gave the area under **Areas Standard Checks** in your latest weekly review, with the change since the week before and your average for the year. See [[06 Reviews]]. Until you write a weekly review of your own, the score shown is Mira's.
- **Going deeper, when you scroll:** your vision for the area, the year week by week (your highest and lowest weeks, with what you wrote), the goals, projects, tasks and habits linked to it, and the ideas from your reading that bear on it. To make a goal appear here, open it and choose the area under **Life Area**. See [[03 Planning]].

You do not need all eight. Use the areas that match your life and leave the others alone.

## The eight areas

### Health

[[0. Health Masterpage]] covers nutrition, fitness and medical records.

- **Nutrition**: [[1. Culinary Database]] holds recipes and ingredients (**➕ New Recipe**, **➕ New Ingredient**), [[2. Meal Plan & Groceries]] plans the week and builds the shopping list, [[3. Health Protocols Tracker]] tracks supplements and protocols.
- **Fitness**: [[1. Training]] holds exercises and routines (**➕ New Exercise**, **➕ New Routine**), [[2. Training plan]] assigns routines to weekdays, [[3. Training log]] shows what you logged.
- **Medical**: [[🩸 0. Bloodwork Masterpage]], [[🩹 0. Injuries Masterpage]], [[🦾 0. Recovery Protocols Masterpage]].

Try first: open one of Mira's routines, [[🏋️ Block 3 - Deadlift day]], then log a session of your own from **Daily Workout** in today's daily note.

### Relationships and social

[[0. Relationships Masterpage]] covers the people in your life and the events you share.

- [[1. Personal CRM]] lists your contacts with the last time you were in touch and the next follow-up (**New Contact**).
- [[2. Event Planner]] holds events you are organising. [[3. Events & Experiences]] holds events you went to.

Try first: open [[Jonas Reuter]] to see a filled contact. Then add one person of your own with **New Contact**, and mention them under **Today's People** in a daily note.

### Business and career

[[0. Career Masterpage]] has three parts.

- **Work**: [[1. Client Operations]] (**New Client**) and [[2. Meetings & Calls]] (**New Meeting**, **New Call**).
- **Content creation**: [[1. Content Strategy]] and [[2. Content Engine]], a board of pieces from idea to published (**New Content**).
- **Academics**: [[1. Courses]] (**✨ Create New**), [[2. Timetable]], [[3. Deadlines]], [[4. Spaced Repetition]], [[5. Research & Citations]] (**➕ Log New Academic Paper**). Courses, Deadlines and Research start on **All phases**. Choose a phase in the **Phase** menu to see one term only. The menu lists the phases you added and every phase a course note carries.

Try first: create one meeting note for your next real meeting with **New Meeting**.

### Finances

[[0. Finances Masterpage]] covers income, expenses and wealth.

- Start with [[7. Finance Settings]]. It holds the categories, accounts and currency that every finance page uses. They are Mira's until you replace them, or until you delete all examples with the switch **Also clear Mira's settings from the system pages** on ([[10 The example files]]). [[1. Live Account Balances]] holds her balances in the same way.
- Log money in the **Daily Ledger** of the daily note, or on [[1. Expense Logger]] and [[1. Income Logger]]. Both write the line into that day's note.
- [[3. Budget Tracker]] creates one budget per month. The button names the month, for example **➕ Create November Budget**. [[2. Bills & Subscriptions]] tracks recurring payments. [[6. Finance Analytics]] charts what you logged.
- [[2. Invoices & Receivables]], [[2. Transfer Logger]], and the three protocol pages for property, shares and crypto are there if you need them.

Try first: replace the accounts in [[7. Finance Settings]], then log one expense from today's daily note.

### Home and environment

[[0. Home Masterpage]] covers the household.

- **Admin**: [[1. House Manual Hub]] keeps the facts you look up twice a year (network, alarm, emergency numbers). [[2. Home Finance & TCO]] and [[3. Spatial Overview]] summarise costs and rooms.
- **Inventory**: [[1. Asset Directory]] lists things you own (**➕ REGISTER NEW ASSET**). [[2. Consumables Hub]] tracks things that run out (**➕ LOG NEW CONSUMABLE**) and feeds [[3. Consumables Restock List]].
- **Maintenance**: [[1. Chore Masterpage]] (**➕ LOG NEW CHORE TASK**), [[2. Repair Hub]] (**➕ LOG NEW REPAIR TASK**), [[3. Home Improvements Hub]] (**➕ LOG NEW PROJECT / WISHLIST**).

Try first: register one appliance with its purchase date in the Asset Directory.

### Personal growth

[[0. Growth Masterpage]] covers how you learn.

- [[0. Knowledge Pipeline]] shows how much sits at each stage from Inbox to permanent notes. See [[05 Knowledge]].
- [[🔭 0. Explorations Masterpage]] holds open questions you are looking into, and [[🧪 0. Protocol Lab Masterpage]] holds experiments you run on yourself. Both use **+ LAUNCH NEW**.
- [[0. Skill Tree]] shows your skills by hours practised.

Try first: read one of Mira's explorations from the Explorations page, then start one of your own on a question you keep coming back to.

### Recreation

[[0. Recreation Masterpage]] covers what you do for its own sake.

- [[1. Hobbies Manager]] (**+ Log New Hobby**) and [[2. Hobbies Gear Manager]] (**➕ REGISTER GEAR**).
- [[1. The Anti-Boredom Engine]] suggests something to do from your own lists. [[2. Recovery Kanban]] sorts activities by the energy and commitment they take (**➕ Add Activity**).
- [[1. Local Places]] and [[2. Recreational Events]] (**🎫 Register Event**).

Try first: open [[🪁 Bouldering]], then log one hobby of your own.

### Spirituality and meaning

[[0. Spirituality Masterpage]] covers inner life, in whatever form yours takes.

- **Mindfulness**: [[1. Meditation Tracker]] reads the meditation time you enter in daily notes. [[2. Cognitive Processing]] is a place to write through a hard thought (**+ New Log**). [[3. Meditation Protocols Manager]] holds practices (**+ Add Protocol**).
- **Philosophy**: [[1. The Existential Framework]].
- **Service**: [[0. Impact Events Masterpage]] (**➕ Log New Impact Event**), [[0. Giving Portfolio]] (**➕ Log New Giving Record**), [[0. Mentorship Hub]] (**➕ Log New Mentorship Profile**). Each page shows four numbers, tabs by type, a search box and its notes as cards.

Try first: write one entry in Cognitive Processing the next time something keeps turning in your head.

## What to expect in the first weeks

- Many cards count the last 7 or 30 days. Mira's notes are all dated 2025, so those counters start at zero and fill as you log your own days. Views that cover all time show her data.
- The **Recent** panel at the bottom of each area page stays empty in this version.
- Amounts are shown in the currency you set in [[7. Finance Settings]]. One exception: the price groups on [[3. Health Protocols Tracker]] (such as "Budget (< $15)") are fixed and written in dollars.

## Area or project?

| It is an area if | It is a project if |
|---|---|
| It has no end date | It has a finish line |
| You keep it at a standard | You complete it |
| "Stay fit" | "Run the spring 10 km" |
| "Keep the flat in order" | "Set up the home office" |

A project usually serves an area. Link the project to a goal, give the goal its **Life Area**, and it shows on that area's page.
