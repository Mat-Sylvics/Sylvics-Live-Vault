# The vault in five minutes

After this page you will know what each of the ten folders is for and where any note goes.

> [!tip] The rule behind this page
> **The Use Rule** and **three modes, three services**: a note lives where it will be used, and each folder is where one kind of work lands. All ten rules: [[The rules of the vault]].

## The ten folders

Open the left sidebar: on a full-screen page click **⛶** first, then click the sidebar icon at the top left, or run `Cmd/Ctrl + P` → **Toggle left sidebar**. You see ten numbered folders. The numbers keep them in order. Do not rename these ten.

| Folder | Plain meaning | What goes in |
|---|---|---|
| `00 - Atlas` | Dashboards | Nothing of yours. This is where you look at everything: the Homepage, the daily pages, the tools, the statistics, the topic maps. |
| `01 - Inbox` | Unsorted | Anything you have just captured and not yet decided about. |
| `02 - Planning` | Things with an end | Who you want to be, goals, habits, skills, projects, tasks, decisions, ideas for later. |
| `03 - Areas` | Things with no end | The parts of life you keep up: health, relationships, work, money, home, growth, recreation, meaning. |
| `04 - Resources` | Raw material | Things other people made, and reference facts: books, articles, videos, tools, people, companies, places, quotes, recipes. |
| `05 - Literature` | Your notes on a source | Your account of one book, article or talk, in your own words. |
| `06 - Permanent` | Your own ideas | One idea per note, with a statement as its title, linked to other ideas. |
| `07 - Journal` | Reflection | Daily notes and weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly reviews. |
| `08 - Archive` | Finished | Completed or dropped projects and anything no longer active. |
| `99 - Meta` | The machinery | Templates, scripts, images, this guide. You rarely need to open it. |

## The one rule

**A note lives where it will be used, not where it came from.** A recipe you cook from is reference material and lives in `04 - Resources`. The plan to cook at home four nights a week is a habit and lives in `02 - Planning`. The dinner you cooked last night is a line in the daily note in `07 - Journal`. One subject, three homes, each where you will look for it.

When you cannot tell yet, the note stays in `01 - Inbox`. That is what the Inbox is for.

## Where does this note go?

Ask these questions in order and stop at the first yes.

1. Is it raw material from someone else: a link, a book, a podcast, a fact? → `04 - Resources`
2. Is it my account of one source? → `05 - Literature`
3. Is it one idea of my own? → `06 - Permanent`
4. Is it something to do or to reach: a task, a project, a goal, a habit? → `02 - Planning`
5. Is it an ongoing part of my life with no finish line? → `03 - Areas`
6. Is it a record of my day or a review? → `07 - Journal`
7. Is it finished or no longer active? → `08 - Archive`
8. Is it about the system itself: a template, a setting? → `99 - Meta`
9. Still not sure? → leave it in `01 - Inbox` and decide at your weekly review.

In practice you rarely file by hand. You create a note with the button on the page for its type, and the button puts it in the right folder with the right layout. [[03 Planning]], [[04 Your life areas]] and [[05 Knowledge]] name those buttons.

## Masterpages

Almost every folder contains a note with "Masterpage" in its name, numbered `0.` or `00.` so that it sorts first. A masterpage is the dashboard for its folder: it lists what is inside, shows counts, and usually carries the button that creates a new note of that type. When you are unsure what a folder is for, open its masterpage.

Leave masterpages where they are. Do not delete, move or rename one, and do not edit the short code block inside it. Other pages open them by name.

## Getting around

| You want | Do this |
|---|---|
| The Homepage | `Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + H` |
| The page that leads to every folder | [[00. Control Center]], or click **Enter the vault →** on the Homepage |
| The ten-minute tour | The green **START HERE** card at the top right of the [[00. Control Center]]. It is there as long as the note [[🚀 Start Here]] exists. |
| Any note by name | `Cmd/Ctrl + O`, then type the name |
| Search the text of all notes | `Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + F` |
| The parent of the page you are on | **UP** at the top of a full-screen page |
| Obsidian's sidebars and tabs back | **⛶** at the top of a full-screen page |

On the Control Center, the ten cards under **2. NAVIGATIONAL HUBS [MASTERPAGES]** open the ten folders.

## The three kinds of work

- **Knowledge**: `04 - Resources` → `05 - Literature` → `06 - Permanent`. Material comes in raw and is refined from left to right. See [[05 Knowledge]].
- **Action**: `02 - Planning` holds what changes and finishes. `03 - Areas` holds what continues. See [[03 Planning]] and [[04 Your life areas]].
- **Reflection**: `07 - Journal`. See [[02 Your day]] and [[06 Reviews]].

The other four folders support these: `00 - Atlas` for seeing, `01 - Inbox` for capturing, `08 - Archive` for storing, `99 - Meta` for the machinery.
