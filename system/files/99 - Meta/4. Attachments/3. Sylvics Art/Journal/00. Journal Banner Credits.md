# Journal banner credits

Every banner in these folders was drawn in code for Sylvics (October 2026) and belongs to Sylvics. No third-party art is used.

- **Daily Notes**: seven places, one per weekday (`MondayBanner` to `SundayBanner`).
- **Weekly Notes**: fifty-three scenes, one per week of the year (`WeeklyBanner1` to `WeeklyBanner53`). The cabin has a room for each month, and each week tells its own small story: New Year, a blizzard, Easter, a picnic, fireworks, Halloween, Christmas and New Year's Eve.
- **Monthly Notes**: twelve views of the same valley through the year (`MonthlyBanner1` to `MonthlyBanner12`, January to December), each with its own moment in the foreground.
- **Quarterly Notes**: the journey to the twin peak, one stage per quarter. Follow the lantern.
- **Yearly Notes**: the view from the top, with the whole year spread across the valley below.

A ginger cat lives in all of them, and a small leaf hexagon is hidden somewhere in each banner. Happy hunting.

To change a banner, replace the file with your own picture of the same name.
