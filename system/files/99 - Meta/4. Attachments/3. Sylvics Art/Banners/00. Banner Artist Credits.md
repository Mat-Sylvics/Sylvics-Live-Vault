# Banners and backgrounds

Every animated banner, room and journal banner in this vault was drawn in code for Sylvics (October and November 2026) and belongs to Sylvics. The eight homepage backgrounds are the exception: they were generated for Sylvics with an AI image tool (see below). There are no third-party illustrations or GIFs in the vault; the previous pictures of unknown origin were removed before the closed beta.

- Banner scenes: `99 - Meta/4. Attachments/3. Sylvics Art/Banners/` (one scene per page, same file names as before).
- Journal banners: `99 - Meta/4. Attachments/3. Sylvics Art/Journal/` (seven daily places, four weekly, twelve monthly views of one valley through the year, four quarterly seasons of the great tree, one yearly).
- Homepage backgrounds: the eight time-of-day scenes `Sylvics Background 1 Midnight.png` to `Sylvics Background 8 Dusk.png` in `Banners/`, animated by the Living homepage. Generated for Sylvics with an AI image tool in July 2026; no third-party picture was used as their source.
- Photographs used as covers elsewhere in the vault are public-domain or Creative Commons images; each cover note names its source.

You may replace any of them with your own pictures: the Vault background setting (Settings → Visuals & UI) and the `Banner` property on a note accept any image in the vault.
