Content_Goals:
  - "[[🎯 Publish 24 issues of Slack in the System]]"
  - "[[🎯 Reach 2,500 newsletter subscribers]]"
Value_Proposition: |-
  **[[📂 Slack in the System - 2025 issues|Slack in the System]]**: one idea from systems thinking or statistics, made usable at work on a Thursday morning.

  About 900 words a fortnight: one idea, one story from work with the names removed, one real question at the end. The letter stays free and stays whole. [[🔍 A paid tier for the newsletter]] waits until after the subscriber goal.
Brand_Archetype: |-
  [[🎭 The Clear Explainer]]: *I make a hard thing plain without making it false.*

  Off limits: jargon as armour; explaining the same point twice because I am unsure of it; a diagram that needs me in the room; making a thing sound more certain than it is so that it lands better.
Tone_And_Voice: |-
  - Plain words, first person, one idea at a time.
  - The example from Tuesday, not the general case.
  - A number comes with its interval, or at least the word *about* ([[📝 Issue 50 - An interval is a kindness|issue 50]]).
  - Cut the first paragraph without mourning it.
  - Simplifying is allowed. Lying by omission is not.
Target_Audiences: |-
  - **Product managers** who own a roadmap and a backlog, and wonder why neither gets shorter.
  - **People who build software for operations**: logistics, dispatch, support queues. They reply with real queues.
  - **Anyone who gives another person a date, a forecast or a percentage.**

  Written for the one reader I have in mind, not for the subscriber count.
Content_Pillars: |-
  - 🎓 **Education**: one tool from systems thinking or statistics, with round numbers. Stocks and flows, base rates, intervals.
  - 🗣️ **Opinion**: a claim about how product work goes wrong, said plainly enough to be argued with.
  - 💡 **Industry Insight**: what operations and freight software teach. Exceptions, queues, the view from a conference.
  - 🚀 **Personal Brand**: my own year in the open. The fortnight I did not write, what I got wrong, the count at the end.
Platforms_Formats: |-
  - 📰 **Newsletter**: every second Thursday at 07:30, scheduled on the Wednesday night. About 900 words, drafted with [[🧭 Newsletter issue from three notes]].
  - 🖋️ **Guest essays**, when someone asks and the outline already exists: *The Product Ledger*, September.
  - 🎤 **Talks**: the Freight Tech Forum, October. One note, said three ways.
  - The own-domain question was priced, answered no and closed.
Content_Rules: |-
  - [[Noor Haddad]] reads every draft before it goes: one cut, one question, one line she liked.
  - One sponsored slot at most: forty words, written by me, labelled, after the essay and never inside it. Nothing I would not use myself ([[🤝 Lumen Roadmaps - newsletter sponsorship|Lumen]], [[🤝 Tern Analytics - newsletter sponsorship|Tern]]).
  - A missed issue is said, not made up. Issue 44 did.
  - When a draft gets hard, the urge to rebuild the platform, the archive or the domain goes on the itch list, not into the week.
