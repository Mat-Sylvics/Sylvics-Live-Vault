# Welcome to the Sylvics vault

This vault is a complete setup for running your life in notes: an Inbox, planning, eight life areas, a knowledge library, a journal and the dashboards that tie them together. It arrives filled with the notes of an invented person, Mira Castellan, so that every page shows something on the first day.

- **New here?** Open [[🚀 Start Here]]. Four small steps, about twenty minutes, and you will have tried the whole loop.
- **Too much at once?** [[🌱 Choose what you see]] shows only the essentials until you want more. Nothing is deleted.
- **Looking for a how-to?** [[00 Guide index]] lists every page of the guide. [[Cheat sheet]] fits on one page.
- **Curious about the examples?** [[About the example files]] says who Mira is and what is invented.
- **Ready to remove examples?** [[Example Files Index]] lists them and lets you remove a note, a folder or all of them. Your own notes are never touched.
