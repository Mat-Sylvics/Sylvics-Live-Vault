# About this beta

You are one of the first people to use Sylvics. This is a closed beta: a small group of testers, before anyone else. Thank you for being here.

## How long it runs

The beta runs for about three weeks. You will get an email at least a week before it ends, so nothing stops by surprise.

Your beta licence unlocks everything in Sylvics for as long as the beta runs.

## Reporting a bug

Write to [sylvicsapp@gmail.com](mailto:sylvicsapp@gmail.com) with:

- What you did, step by step.
- What happened.
- What you expected to happen.
- A screenshot.
- Your system (macOS, Windows or Linux) and your Obsidian version, shown under **Settings → General**.

One problem per email is the easiest to follow. [[12 Troubleshooting and FAQ#Reporting a problem|Reporting a problem]] in the guide says where the error log is and what never to send.

## What helps most

- Your first impressions. They are the hardest to get back later.
- Where you got stuck, or had to guess what to do.
- What you would miss if Sylvics went away, and what you never used.

Short notes are welcome. One sentence written when it happens is worth more than a long report written a week later.

## Your privacy

Nothing in your vault is sent anywhere. AI is off unless you turn it on with your own key. What you send by email is used only to improve Sylvics.

Thank you for testing. Every report makes Sylvics better for the next person who opens it.

## About Sage and the examples

Sage, the assistant in the sidebar, is off until you turn it on, and when it is on it sends only what you allow to the provider you chose. It can be wrong: check anything that matters before you act on it. The example notes, numbers and plans in this vault belong to Mira Castellan, a fictional person; nothing in them is advice of any kind.
